# Enterprise mode

Use for sensitive or high-risk work. Apply Professional plus explicit threat model, migration rehearsal on disposable data, performance budgets, independent QA, UAT, documented rollback and owner approval. Missing required independence or evidence is BLOCKED.

Follow `../references/workflows.md` for lifecycle and `../quality-gates/gates.md` for gate decisions. Choose scope FULL, FEATURE, REDESIGN, COMPONENT or FIX separately. Do not infer deployment permission.
